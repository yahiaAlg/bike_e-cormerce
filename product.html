Shipping & Returns

Delivery

In-stock motorcycles are delivered within 5–7 business days. Pre-order models are estimated at 4–6 weeks. Motorcycle delivery is free within the EU. You will receive a tracking number via email once your order ships.

Returns

You have 14 days from delivery to return any ARKO product. Motorcycles must have less than 50 km on the odometer. Accessories must be unused and in original packaging. Return shipping is free for orders within the EU.

Warranty

Every ARKO motorcycle includes a 2-year warranty on the bike and a 3-year warranty on the battery. Extended coverage up to 5 years is available for \u20ac790.
