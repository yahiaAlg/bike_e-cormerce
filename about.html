THE SILENCE

Founded in 2021 in Berlin, ARKO builds electric motorcycles for riders who go where the road ends. No noise. No emissions. No compromise.

The Mission

Terrain Doesn't Forgive. Neither Do We.

We started ARKO because electric motorcycles were either street scooters or expensive prototypes. We wanted a bike that could handle a hard enduro stage on Saturday and commute to work on Monday — all electric, all silent, all torque.

Every ARKO is designed and assembled in our Berlin facility. We forge our own frames, wind our own motors, and test every bike on the trails of the Brandenburg forest before it ships.

We believe electric is not a compromise — it's an advantage. Instant torque. Zero maintenance. Silent operation that lets you hear the trail instead of the engine.

What We Believe

Our Values

Electric First

We don't make gas bikes and add a battery. Every ARKO is designed from the ground up as an electric machine — frame, motor, and battery as one system.

Built to Last

Aircraft-grade aluminium, sealed bearings, and IP67-rated electronics. Our bikes are overbuilt because we'd rather you ride than visit a workshop.

Zero Compromise

Zero emissions doesn't mean zero performance. We prove that electric can outperform combustion on the trail, on the road, and on the track.

The Journey

Our Timeline

2021

The Idea

Co-founders Erik Volker and Mia Brandt sketch the first ARKO concept on a napkin in a Berlin coffee shop. The goal: an electric enduro that actually works.

2022

First Prototype

The ARKO-0 prototype completes its first forest test. 25 kW, 80 km range, and a frame forged in a rented workshop in Kreuzberg.

2023

Series A & Berlin Factory

€12M Series A funding. We move into our Berlin-Mitte production facility and begin small-batch production of the RVX.

2024

RVX Launch & First Dealers

The ARKO RVX ships to early customers. We open our first three dealers in Berlin, Munich, and Amsterdam.

2025

Full Fleet & EU Expansion

The Trail, Adventure, and Performance lines launch. We expand to 8 dealers across 6 European countries.

2026

Performance RS & Beyond

The limited-edition Performance RS sells out in 48 hours. We announce plans for North American expansion in 2027.
